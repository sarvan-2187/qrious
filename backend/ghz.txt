```text
q0: |0⟩ ──H────■───────── M
               │
q1: |0⟩ ───────X────■──── M
                    │
q2: |0⟩ ────────────X──── M
```

Alternatively, $q_0$ can act as the control for both CNOTs (a star/fan-out topology), which is mathematically identical but may be compiled differently on physical hardware depending on qubit connectivity.
